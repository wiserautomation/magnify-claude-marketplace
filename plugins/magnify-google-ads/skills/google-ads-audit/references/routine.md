# Review routine

Consistent hygiene beats big changes. Changing too often is as harmful as not optimising.

| When | What | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily (only when asked) | Spend vs budget, sudden conversion drop (tracking or feed), disapproved products or ads, hero stock | `get_account_overview` (1) |
| Weekly | Search terms (Search, Shopping, PMax), negatives, budget-limited campaigns, targets moved 10-15% at most, PMax channel mix | `get_search_terms` (4), `diagnose_account` (2) |
| Every 2 weeks | Heroes, villains and zombies; custom labels; title tests; device and location performance | shopping-pmax skill |
| Monthly | PMax assets (replace the weakest), RSAs, extensions, price competitiveness, Quality Score bands (under 3, 3-7, 8-10), auction insights, Google's recommendations (apply only what makes sense) | `get_performance` (2), `get_opportunities` (3) |
| Quarterly | Full audit: structure, tracking vs the store's sales, bidding strategy, break-even ROAS from real margins, incrementality tests, season plan | Quick audit + full audit |

Mistakes a professional never makes:
- Optimising platform ROAS without looking at margin or total store sales.
- Changing bid, budget and structure in the same week.
- Letting PMax claim brand and remarketing sales and calling it growth.
- Accepting Google's automatic recommendations without review.
- Splitting the account into so many campaigns that none reaches 30 conversions a month.
- Blaming the ads for a price, stock or website problem.
