# Symptom trees

Walk each tree top to bottom. Do not move to the next question until the previous one is answered. Every step names the cheapest read that answers it.

## Conversions dropped suddenly
1. Tracking broken? `diagnose_conversion_setup`. If yes: fix the tag; exclude the broken days from bidding data (data exclusion).
2. Feed disapproved or products out of stock? Merchant Center health (shopping-pmax skill).
3. Site or checkout down, price changed, stock of hero products? Ask the user: Magnify cannot see the site's checkout.
4. Only then: a bid or budget change in the last 14 days? Undo or wait out the learning window.

## ROAS below break-even
1. Tracking reliable? (as above)
2. Junk search terms? Negate patterns (negative-keywords skill).
3. Villain products spending without sales? Review price, title, image, landing page; then exclude or move to a strict target.
4. Price not competitive (more than ~10% above the benchmark)? A price problem no ad change fixes.
5. PMax spending on Display or YouTube without results? Exclusions, better creative or feed-only PMax.
6. Then raise tROAS 10-15%.

## Not spending the budget
Target too strict (lower tROAS 10%), few eligible products (feed disapprovals), or little demand (widen to generic Search or Demand Gen).

## ROAS above target
- Limited by budget: raise budget 15-20% every 3-7 days.
- Not limited: lower tROAS 10% to buy volume.

## CPC rising
Auction insights for new competitors or season; Quality Score (tighter ad groups, specific landing page); broad match without control. Add or tighten a target if CPA rose more than 20% at a flat conversion rate.

## Lots of traffic, few sales
Landing page speed, checkout, price, shipping cost. Fix conversion rate and offer before touching bids.

## PMax spends on a few products
The algorithm favours products with history. Move the ignored products to their own PMax or Shopping campaign with its own budget.

## Good ROAS, total sales flat
Brand and remarketing cannibalisation. Brand exclusion on PMax, a new-customer goal, an incrementality test; judge by total store sales over ad spend (MER), which needs the user's own sales figure.

## Always in learning
Too many changes or too few conversions per campaign. Consolidate campaigns; change one lever every 1-2 weeks.
