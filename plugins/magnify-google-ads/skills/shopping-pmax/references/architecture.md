# Architecture by scenario

Too many campaigns on a small budget split the data and Smart Bidding never learns. Each campaign with Smart Bidding needs about 30-50 conversions a month.

| Scenario | Monthly budget (indicative) | Structure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small store or launch | under ~1,500 | Brand Search (if there is brand demand) + one Standard Shopping with all products on capped Maximize Clicks; after 4-8 weeks, feed-only PMax or full PMax on heroes |
| Growing store | ~1,500-10,000 | Brand Search + PMax heroes (tROAS) + PMax or Shopping sidekicks + low-CPC Shopping for zombies + generic Search on top categories; remarketing or Demand Gen optional |
| Large catalogue | over ~10,000 | Several PMax by margin or category (each able to reach 30-50 conversions a month) + Standard Shopping for query control + Search by category and long tail + Dynamic Search Ads + Demand Gen/YouTube + brand |
| Strong brand demand | any | Brand Search always separate; brand excluded on PMax so its real prospecting shows |
| Few high-ticket products | any | Exact/phrase Search per product + Shopping per product + PMax with an asset group per product |

## Launch order for a new account
1. Week 0: tracking, Merchant Center, feed, account exclusions and negative lists.
2. Week 1: brand Search + Standard Shopping (or PMax on a small budget for simplicity) without a target.
3. Weeks 2-6: clean search terms, remove villains, improve hero titles.
4. Around 30 conversions a month: move to tROAS or tCPA and create hero/sidekick/zombie segments.
5. With winning queries: generic and long-tail Search.
6. With spare budget and stable ROAS: Demand Gen or YouTube to create demand.

## Standard Shopping or Performance Max
| Standard Shopping when | PMax when |
| --- | --- |
| You need fine control of queries and bids per product | 30+ conversions a month and you want to scale |
| Thin margins or expensive CPCs | You have good images and video |
| You want cheap long tail (zombies) | You want YouTube, Discover and Gmail with dynamic remarketing |
| You are testing titles and need clean query data | The catalogue is too large to manage groups by hand |

The same product in Shopping and PMax competes for Ad Rank. Only do it on purpose, as a test. Next to PMax, a Standard Shopping campaign on low manual CPC is valuable mainly for its search-terms report.
