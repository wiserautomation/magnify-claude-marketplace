# Performance Max

## Structure
- With more than ~50 products and enough budget, segment PMax by custom label (heroes, sidekicks, villains/zombies) or by margin, never one PMax for everything. Each PMax must be able to reach 30 conversions a month; otherwise merge.
- Asset groups by category, with the listing group filtered to that category, so creative and signals match the products.
- One audience-signal type per asset group (search themes from winning queries and competitor names; own customer lists and site visitors; interests or in-market), so you can see which works and switch off the one that does not. Adding an asset group restarts learning only for that group.
- Feed-only PMax when there is no good creative.
- In a single PMax, do not exclude products without a reason: dynamic remarketing uses the products of the campaign that brought the click.

## Assets (replace the weakest every 4-6 weeks)
- Per asset group: 5 short headlines, 5 long headlines, 5 descriptions, 15-20 images in landscape 1.91:1, square 1:1 and portrait 4:5, logos and 1-3 own videos (without them Google makes low-quality ones). Treat every "optional" asset as required.
- Headlines with concrete selling points (free shipping from X, 30-day returns, guarantee, review count), written with the copywriting skill.
- AI text and final URL expansion off; if URL expansion stays on, exclude blog, contact, cart, login and policy pages.

## Channels (weekly)
- If Display or YouTube take more than 20-30% of cost with very poor ROAS: account-level app and placement exclusions, better creative, or feed-only PMax.
- A channel with low direct ROAS may assist later searches: check total sales trends before cutting hard.

## Search terms and brand
- Read PMax search terms; add campaign-level negatives or shared lists.
- Brand exclusion on PMax when there is a brand Search campaign, so PMax does not claim easy brand sales.
- Exclude customer lists in prospecting PMax when the goal is only new customers.

## Signals and insights
Signals are a starting point, not a restriction. Refresh search-theme signals with recent winning queries. In Insights, a segment with an unexpected share of conversions deserves its own asset group and creative.

## Quick tree
- High ROAS but mostly brand or remarketing: brand exclusion, check new-customer share, incrementality test.
- Spends on Display or YouTube without converting: exclusions, creative or feed-only.
- Does not spend: tROAS too high, disapprovals, few eligible products or weak asset groups.
- Spends on a few products: move the ignored ones to their own campaign with its own budget.
- Performance drops after changes: let learning run 1-2 weeks; change one lever at a time.
