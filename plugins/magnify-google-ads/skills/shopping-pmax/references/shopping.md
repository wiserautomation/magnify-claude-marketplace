# Standard Shopping

## Search terms (weekly; daily in the first month or large accounts)
- Negate irrelevant themes as phrase or exact patterns (negative-keywords skill).
- A relevant term is negated only after cost >= 1.5-2x target CPA with 0 conversions, and after checking title and price.
- Terms with 2+ conversions at or above target: add as exact/phrase keywords in Search and put the keyword into the product title.

## Query sculpting (advanced, manual CPC or Maximize Clicks on thin margins)
Three campaigns with the same products: generic (high priority, low bid, brand and model negatives), brand/category (medium priority, model negatives), specific model (low priority, high bid). It pays more for specific queries. With Smart Bidding and high volume it adds little.

## Products (every two weeks)
- Classify heroes, sidekicks, villains and zombies (main skill, section 2).
- Monthly: product groups down to item id, sorted by clicks; exclude items with 50+ clicks and ROAS below the minimum, raise bids on those above.

## Titles
Highest-volume converting keyword first; searched attributes (size, color, material, compatibility, quantity). Test new titles on half the products against control for 2-4 weeks, measuring impressions and CTR.

## Devices, locations, schedules
- With Smart Bidding, no device bid adjustments except -100%, and only when a device spent 2-3x target CPA without converting.
- Exclude regions without delivery or with chronically poor ROAS.
- Search partners off if their ROAS is clearly lower.

## Quick tree
- ROAS below break-even: tracking OK? junk queries (negate)? villains (exclude)? price not competitive? then raise tROAS 10-15%.
- Above target and limited by budget: +15-20% budget every 3-7 days. Above target and not limited: lower tROAS 10%.
- Few impressions: titles and feed, disapprovals, target too high, budget.
- Low CTR: main image, price, promotions, ratings.
