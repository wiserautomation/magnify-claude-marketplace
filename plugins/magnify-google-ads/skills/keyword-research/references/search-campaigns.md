# Search campaigns for ecommerce

In ecommerce, Search complements Shopping and PMax: it captures high-intent queries with a tailored message and landing page, and protects the brand.

## Brand
- Separate campaign, brand in exact and phrase; a high target or low manual CPC.
- Brand negated in generic Search and excluded on PMax.
- Brand pinned in headline 1; the rest are selling points the competition lacks.
- Sitelinks to top categories and offers. Visitor audiences in observation.
- Monthly auction insights for competitors bidding on the brand. With no one bidding, test lowering brand spend and watch total sales.

## Generic by category
- One campaign or ad group per profitable category (from Shopping/PMax data), phrase and exact match.
- Landing page = the filtered category page, never the home page.
- Broad match with Smart Bidding only after ~30 conversions a month and a good negative list.

## Long tail from winning terms
- Take Shopping/PMax/Search terms with 2+ conversions and ROAS at or above target.
- Tight ad groups (one theme, 1-5 exact/phrase keywords) with their own ad and landing page.
- Patterns: model + size or color, buy + model, model + price, alternative to X.
- Optionally add the same terms as negatives in the generic campaign to route the traffic to the tailored group.
- Few high-ticket products: nearly one ad group per product.

## Dynamic Search Ads
- Only with URL rules that target product and category pages; exclude blog, out-of-stock, policy, cart and login pages; brand negated. AI Max for Search stays off by default.
- Harvest winning queries into keyword ad groups; negate the bad ones; review the landing-page report.

## Competitors
- Only when the margin allows and the offer is clearly better (price, shipping, guarantee). Separate campaign, small budget, exact match only.
- No competitor trademark in headlines: talk about the product they want. Landing page: the product you share, or the closest one.
- No call extension. Expect low Quality Score and high CPC.

## Quick tree
- Low Quality Score: tighter ad groups, specific landing page, CTR.
- High CPC and low ROAS: stricter match types, negatives, landing page, or leave the query to Shopping.
- Lots of broad-match volume without conversions: back to phrase and exact until there is data.
